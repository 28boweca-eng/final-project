The Website for All Your Neurodivergent Traveling Questions

Let's face it. Traveling in an airport is a living nightmare at times. But for some people, it's utterly unbearable. So that's where this site comes in. The Neurodivergent's Guide to the Airport is a comprehensive guide to navigating an airport, as well as packing, dealing with being on an airplane, and so much more. It was designed by a neurodivergent person with other people like themselves in mind, and hopefully, it can help at least one person make in through an airport safely and comfortably.
