Getting into the Airport

Finding Parking

When finding parking for your car, it's important to consider your options as to where you want to put it for the duration of your vacation.

Oftentimes, airports have their own parking garages, and sometimes even have parking spots right next to the entrance. However, these parking garages are expensive, and might not be nearly as convenient as they seem.

Third party parking companies, such as FastPark and ValAir, are also readily available. These places are significantly cheaper than parking at the airport, but are further away from the airport, so you may need to take one of the parking company's buses to get to the airport.

Of course, there is always the option of just leaving your car at home and taking an Uber or Lyft. While this is arguably the cheapest method, it can be challenging for families traveling together, as there aren't a ton of Ubers that can fit more than 4 people, let alone a ton of baggage.

Dropping Off Checked Bags

When traveling with checked bags, the only space where they can go is under the plane. This means that you will have to go to your airline's designated checked bag drop off area. However, lines for the drop off areas can get pretty long, especially during holiday seasons. Sadly, this is really the only way to get a larger bag on the plan, so you will have to wait.

Once you reach the drop off area, put your checked bag on the scale to weigh it. If you are an adult, you will be asked to show your ID. It's worth noting that airports only accept airport compliant ID's, and if you have just a standard ID, you may not be able to travel on airplanes. As soon as you show your ID, the agent will tag your bags, and put it on the conveyor belt to be sent under the plane.

Going Through Security

Just like when dropping off checked bags, you may have to wait in egregiously long lines to get to security, especially during holidays. Unfortunately, when this happens, the only thing you can really do is wait it out. If you are in this situation, feel free to do anything to occupy yourself without annoying everyone else in line. Listen to some music with your headphones, get out some snacks, or play with quieter fidget toys. Don’t cut in line though, as people will get very upset if you do that.

Once you get up to the security station, you will be told to place your bags in a designated bin so that they can go through the X-ray machine for inspection. The workers will also prompt you to remove your electronic devices and liquids, and put them in the same bin. You may also be told to remove your shoes if you are over the age of 14. Once all of this is done, you will be directed to either the metal detector, or the X-ray scanner if you have certain medical devices, such as an insulin pump. It is very important to listen to the TSA agents, as they can prevent you from getting on your flight if you don’t comply.

It should be known that there are certain things you can and cannot bring through security, while other things also have to go through additional screening. Oftentimes, contraband items will just be confiscated, and you will be given a warning. However, if you bring in something really dangerous, such as a knife or a gun, you may be marked as potentially dangerous, and have to go through extra security, or even be banned from flying depending on what you brought.
